02 · How the tags work

A need-first filing system

The old domains still matter, but they are not the filing cabinet. These tags tell us what doubt the proof closes, how strong the evidence is, who recognizes themselves in it, and where it belongs.

JOB

The one primary function this person performs in the argument.

NEED

The buyer question or emotional doubt this testimonial answers.

PROOF

The evidence form: timing, behavior, comparison, outside observation, or sustained use.

AUDIENCE

The practice world or buyer most likely to trust and recognize the person.

STATUS + RISK

Permission, disclosure, relationship, and claim boundaries that travel with the proof.

ASSET + USE

What media exists and the strongest current moment or surface for the proof.
