02How it works

A birth chart goes in. Four numbers come out. The music carries them.

01

The chart is the input.

You give your exact birth date, time, and place. Zodiac reads the raw planetary positions from that moment, rather than a sun sign or a horoscope reading.

02

Four frequencies come out.

Core, Love, Vitality, and Abundance. One number each, somewhere between 250 Hz and 950 Hz. They are set at your birth and they stay put while everything else about you changes.

03

The music carries them.

Composed tracks with your frequencies inside them. You listen while you work, walk, cook, or sleep. Eyes open, nothing to practice, nothing to interpret.

03Who this is for

It suits some people and not others. Here is the honest version.

Probably you, if

  • You already believe your chart says something true about you, and you are tired of reading about it instead of living it.
  • You want the experience rather than another interpretation to study.
  • You will actually play it most days for a few months and let it work in the background.

Probably not you, if

  • You want a horoscope, a reading, or predictions. Zodiac gives you none of those. The chart is the input, not the product.
  • You are looking for treatment for a medical or mental-health condition. This is music, and it is not that.
  • You want one dramatic session. It is built for accumulation across a year, not for a single sitting.

I don’t know my exact birth time. Can I still use it?

Yes. This is our most common question, so you are not alone. Your birth time does fine-tune one part of how we calculate your frequencies, so the closer it is, the better. But depending on your configuration, being off by a few minutes, or even a couple of hours, often will not change your calculation much. The exact time is ideal. If yours is an estimate, give us your best one and we calculate from there.

Time moves one hand on the dial. The date and the place hold the rest of the chart in place.

04Zodiac’s story

Zodiac began with a debunking that failed.

Astrologers described things our founder had spent eight years in therapy uncovering. They had never met him. They were working only from his birth chart.

He came from the evidence-based world and he assumed he could take it apart. He could not. So he went looking for the source instead, which led to a sound lineage in India, Sri Vidya and Mantra Sastra, and to a long collaboration with the people inside it.

The algorithm that came out of that work is what turns a chart into four frequencies. We have watched it produce the same kind of result for years.

We still do not claim to fully understand how it lands on the right numbers.
