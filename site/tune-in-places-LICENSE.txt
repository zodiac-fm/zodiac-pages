Tune In 06 offline place suggestions

Source: the IANA Time Zone Database tables zone.tab and iso3166.tab, as
installed at /usr/share/zoneinfo on the build machine. Both source files
state that they are in the public domain (Arthur David Olson, 2009-05-17).

The JSON contains 418 representative place names derived from timezone
identifiers, plus country names from iso3166.tab. Underscores were changed
to spaces. It is a small suggestion list, not a comprehensive gazetteer.
Users may enter any town or city without choosing a suggestion.

IANA Time Zone Database: https://www.iana.org/time-zones
