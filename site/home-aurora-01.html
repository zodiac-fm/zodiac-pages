Who this is for

Made for people who already trust their chart.

You have had the readings. You have done the work. You know the shape of yourself on paper, and there is still a distance between knowing it and living it. That distance is where this sits.

Made for you if

  • You already believe your chart says something true.
  • You want an experience rather than another interpretation.
  • You will give it a few ordinary weeks of listening.

Probably not for you if

  • You are looking for readings, transits, or a daily horoscope. We do not do those.

Zodiac's story

We tried to prove it wrong.

Zodiac did not start as a music company. It started with a measurement that had no business holding up, so we spent a long time trying to knock it down. We changed the inputs. We handed it to people with every reason to laugh at it. It kept doing the same thing.

We are not astrologers and we are not gurus. We are the people who found this, kept testing it, and are still a little surprised by what comes back. That is the honest version, and it is the only one we tell.
