Who this is for

03

Who this is for, honestly.

Most of the people here already believe their chart says something true about them. They have done the readings, the courses, the practice. Zodiac is usually not the first thing they have tried, and it is rarely the thing they expected to work.

It is also worth saying who it is not for. If you want a meditation app, a daily horoscope, or something to treat a medical condition, this is not that. It is music, and it asks almost nothing of you, which is exactly why some people bounce off it.

If you don’t know your birth time

You can still use Zodiac. Your birth time fine-tunes one part of the calculation, so the closer it is the better, but depending on your configuration, being off by a few minutes, or even a couple of hours, often will not change much.

Give us your best estimate. You can correct it later, and we will recalculate.

Zodiac’s story

04

Where Zodiac came from.

Michael Lovitch came out of the evidence-based world. A master’s in academic hypnosis, a company built on publishing the work of clinical hypnotists, training with Robert Dilts, eight years of his own therapy. He was a materialist, and he assumed everything came down to the power of the mind.

Then astrologers told him what those eight years had uncovered, without ever having met him, working only from his birth chart. He tried to knock it down and could not.

So he went looking for the source. It turned out to be an old Indian sound lineage, and Zodiac’s algorithm came out of that work. We still cannot fully explain how it lands on the right frequencies, and we would rather say that than pretend otherwise. Nobody here is a guru.
