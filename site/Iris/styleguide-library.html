Part Two

EVERYTHING TO BUILD A PAGE

The bank alone is not the styleguide. Part One says which frame. Part Two says how a page is made out of them — the filter every frame passes, the light and texture laws, the furniture, the proof, the grammar, and the job-by-job call sheet.

Chapter 1

THE FILTER — THE THIRTEEN

Every frame passes these before it ships. The numbers are the floor; rule 13 is the gate.

  1. Kill the colour and look — if nothing holds your eye in grey, colour will not rescue it.
  2. The coloured thing must stand out even in grey.
  3. When a colour looks weak, fix the grey behind it — never the saturation.
  4. Never desaturate, always convert — keep the colour master.
  5. Colour sits on top of the photograph's own light — a flat inside is paint.
  6. Point at what is making the colour — no source, no colour.
  7. Quiet everything, let one thing sing.
  8. Stand close — the edge gives a fake away.
  9. Same texture on both sides of the line.
  10. Real colour lands on what sits nearby.
  11. Write the recipe down or it will not happen twice.
  12. Make a calmer version and let them fight.
  13. Name the light on a person — colour may kiss skin, never paint it.
Chapter 2

LIGHT

  1. Start bright — frames and pages open in light.
  2. Light is made into the photo at creation, never painted after.
  3. Every light has a pointable source.
  4. Faces take warm key light only; a real reflection may kiss skin; frequency colour may never paint it.
  5. Amber is our sunlight, not a pop.
Chapter 3

TEXTURE

  1. Texture is photographed, never tiled.
  2. Busy carries no words — copy sits on a calm zone, always.
  3. One grain, frame and page; type is the one exemption.
  4. Texture change announces the section — no divider lines.
  5. Sharpness is hierarchy — crisp only where the eye should land.
Chapter 4

FURNITURE charts, bullets, captions

Master law
Colour lives in the mark, not the font — coloured words are a rare deliberate move, on Michael's taste, never a habit.
Charts
  • Ink on paper.
  • A bar wears band colour only when it IS that frequency.
  • Numbers in Switzer, never the serif.
  • One chart style per screen.
  • Ground stays paper, grid whisper-grey.
Bullets
  • A band-coloured mark at full intensity, ink words.
Captions
  • Quiet ink under the photo; a small chip only when the photo belongs to a frequency.
Chapter 5

PROOF testimonials

  1. Faces pure silver, full contrast, complete crowns, never coloured, never retouched.
  2. Colour beside the person, never on them — the frequency chip by the kicker.
  3. Full names always; claims may ride.
  4. Ground quiet paper — the calmest room in the house.
Chapter 6

PAGE GRAMMAR

  1. SILVER is the ground everything sits on; the valley that makes pops detonate.
  2. One SOLO per section — the section's voice.
  3. THROUGH and SPILL are how a SOLO proves it is real.
  4. WORN is the only people move; SUN is the hour, riding every treatment.
  5. CHORD bookends only — opener and finale, never the middle.
  6. CHIP speaks wherever the product does.
  7. The ground serves the section's intent — night, sun, or neutral, anywhere — and never screws with the pop (the ground-pop law above).
