Section 01

Partners / Dark Post and Delist

Paid ads that run under a mini-celebrity’s handle without ever touching their public presence: dark posts on Meta, unlisted (delisted) videos on YouTube.

The Dark Post Model

  • What it is: Paid ads running under the mini-celebrity’s handle (@MiniCelebrity).
  • Where it lives: Exclusively inside targeted user feeds. It never touches or clutters their public Instagram grid, Facebook timeline, or YouTube channel homepage.
  • Who controls what: The creator gives one-time account approval and uploads unlisted files. The brand edits all media, writes all copy, sets ad budgets, controls targeting, and manages DM automation via ManyChat.

Approval Scripts — Self-Managed Creators (Phone)

Script A: Meta approval (send via IG DM / email)

Subject: Quick 30-sec ad approval for our campaign 🚀

Hey [Creator Name]!

To run our upcoming ads under your handle, we use Meta’s official Partnership Ads tool.

🔒 Reassurance: This is an “ads-only” permission. It will not post anything to your public Instagram grid or feed. We run these purely as background “dark posts”.

30-second setup:

  1. Make sure your IG is a Creator or Business account (Settings → Account type → Switch to Professional).
  2. Open Instagram → go to your Profile → tap the Menu (≡) in the top right.
  3. Tap Creator tools and controls (or Business tools).
  4. Tap Partnership ads → Ad partners.
  5. Under Pending Requests, look for @[YourBrandHandle] and tap Approve.

Once approved, we handle everything else! Let me know when it’s done 🙌

Script B: YouTube unlisted upload (send via DM / email)

Subject: YouTube unlisted video setup

Hey [Creator Name]!

For YouTube, we’re running our ads natively from your channel so it appears as an organic feature.

🔒 Reassurance: The video will be set to Unlisted. It will not notify your subscribers or show up on your channel homepage.

Two steps:

  1. Link Google Ads: In YouTube Studio desktop, go to Settings → Channel → Advanced → Link Account, enter our Google Ads ID [XXX-XXX-XXXX], and hit Save.
  2. Upload the video: Upload the edited video we sent you, set Visibility to UNLISTED, then send us the video URL.

Approval Scripts — Managers & Agencies (Desktop)

At this level the artist isn’t tapping around on their phone — a digital manager, PR agency, or social media director handles everything from a desktop via Meta Business Suite / Business Manager. Desktop-native versions:

Meta — Option A: the 1-click “Requests” approval (fastest)

Send after you trigger the request from your Ads Manager:

To [Manager/Agency Name]

To approve our Partnership Ad request on desktop so we can run our campaign under @[ArtistHandle] without touching your public feed:

  1. Go to business.facebook.com on a desktop browser.
  2. Click Settings (the gear icon at the bottom left).
  3. Click Requests in the left sidebar menu (under the Business Portfolio section).
  4. Go to the Received tab, find the pending request from [Your Brand / Business Manager Name], and click Approve.

This grants our ad account background access to launch dark posts using the handle, leaving the main profile grid completely untouched.

Meta — Option B: sharing partner access via Business ID (if they initiate)

If their agency prefers to push access to you directly, give them your 15-digit Meta Business Portfolio ID (Business Settings → Business Info):

To [Manager/Agency Name]

  1. Go to business.facebook.com → Settings → Partners.
  2. Click Add → Give a partner access to your assets.
  3. Enter our Meta Business ID: [YOUR-15-DIGIT-BUSINESS-ID].
  4. Under Assets, select @[ArtistHandle] (Instagram account & Facebook page).
  5. Toggle ON Create / Manage Ads (Partnership Ads) and click Save Changes.

YouTube — the manager version (they still upload)

Locked rule: on YouTube, the creator’s side always uploads — never us. The video must live on their channel to keep channel authority, the Creator Partnership Ad classification, and the viewer Data Segments. An earlier brand-uploads-via-Editor-access draft is superseded.

To [Manager/Agency Name]

For YouTube, Google’s ad engine requires the video to be uploaded natively by @[ArtistChannel] so the ad retains full channel authority and subscriber signals.

🔒 This will not touch your channel grid or notify subscribers.

Instructions (takes 60 seconds):

  1. Log into studio.youtube.com on desktop and upload the video file we provided.
  2. Set the Visibility to UNLISTED.
  3. Under the video settings / permissions tab, select Link to Google Ads (or generate a Shared Video Code).
  4. Enter our 10-digit Google Ads ID [XXX-XXX-XXXX] and send us the video URL.

Once sent, our media team handles all ad bidding, targeting, and traffic management from our dashboard.

What We Do: Creative & Campaign Execution

Step 1 — Asset collection

Ask the creator for raw, unedited footage or high-res photos via Google Drive/Dropbox (holding headphones, reacting to an audio clip, talking about tuning their music).

Step 2 — In-house production & editing

  • Video hooks: Chop the raw video into 3–5 different 3-second hook variations.
  • On-screen overlays: Captions, dynamic audio visualizers, screen recordings of the 250–950 Hz frequency dial inside zodiac.fm.
  • Static assets: High-contrast quote cards (“Why @Artist uses Zodiac.fm”), text-message screenshots, 4:5 vertical photo ads.
  • Aspect ratios: 9:16 (Reels/Shorts/Stories) and 4:5 (Feed).

Step 3 — Launching on Meta Ads Manager

  1. Create a campaign (Objective: Sales or App Promotion).
  2. At the Ad Level, toggle ON Partnership Ad and select the creator’s identity.
  3. Select Create Ad (keeps it an unpublished dark post).
  4. Upload the edited video/photo asset.
  5. Set the destination: Option 1 — landing page at zodiac.fm/artistname. Option 2 — CTA “Send Instagram Message”, Partner App → ManyChat, automated bot flow delivers app links inside DMs.

Step 4 — Launching on YouTube (Google Ads)

  1. Grab the unlisted video URL uploaded by the creator.
  2. Open Google Ads → create a Video Campaign (sub-type: Drive Conversions or Shorts).
  3. Paste the unlisted YouTube URL into the ad setup.
  4. Set the CTA button to “Download” or “Listen Now” pointing to the zodiac.fm landing page.
  5. Launch. The ad displays the creator’s avatar and handle while Google Ads manages traffic, bidding, and conversions.

Section 02

Creator Sourcing & Recruitment

Skip the traditional influencer agency ($5,000–$15,000/month retainers to make phone calls). Since we handle all ad execution, media buying, and editing in-house, we only need a partner for sourcing, outreach, and signed agreements.

Option 1 — Whitelisting Marketplaces (lowest cost & fastest)

Platforms built to recruit creators who already know how to grant Meta Partnership Ad and YouTube permissions.

Insense.pro

Post a brief for musicians, ambient creators, or string players willing to grant Partnership Ad access; creators apply or get matched. Built-in workflow specifically for ad whitelisting — creators know going in they’re granting ad account access. Roughly $300–$400/month platform fee, or pay-per-campaign.

Collabstr

Free-to-browse marketplace, searchable by niche (Musicians, Wellness, Instrumentalists). No subscription — pay only when you hire. Message creators directly: “We pay $X for 1 raw video + Partnership Ad access.”

Option 2 — Commission-Based Creator Scouts (best value)

Instead of a retainer, hire a freelance creator scout on a bounty model (pay-per-signed-artist):

  1. Search Upwork, Fiverr Pro, or LinkedIn for “Influencer Outreach Specialist”, “Music Talent Scout”, or “Creator Partnerships Manager”.
  2. Offer a performance hybrid: $200–$500/month base to cover hours/tools, plus a $50–$100 bounty for every artist who signs the license agreement and completes the Meta/YouTube permission setup.

Fixed agency cost becomes a variable performance cost: the scout spends all day DMing talent and emailing managers; real money moves only when an artist actually grants access.
