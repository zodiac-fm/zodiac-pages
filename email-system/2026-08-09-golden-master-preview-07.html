Hi Michael,

There is no perfect way to listen to Zodiac. The useful way is the one that slips naturally into your life and gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

You can sit still, close your eyes, and make it a practice. But you can also listen while you walk, cook, tidy up, travel, or settle in for the night.

Let the music meet the moment

You do not have to concentrate on every note. Choose a soundscape that suits the moment, press play, and continue with what you were already doing.

A walk, a commute, the kitchen, the gym, or the last few minutes before sleep can all become listening time. It does not need to look like a formal practice.

Think in weeks, not perfect days

Begin with at least nine minutes a day on average. Then let your listening move across all four frequencies over the week: Core, Love, Vitality, and Abundance.

  1. Open your Playlist and choose what fits the moment.
  2. Listen for at least nine minutes.
  3. Use Frequency Balance to see what your week needs next.
Playlist holds the tracks you want close by.
Frequency Balance shows what your week needs next.

If you miss a day, you have not ruined anything. The practical floor is measured across the week, and each frequency simply needs a place in that week.

Build a Playlist you will actually use

Start with the tracks you already enjoy. Saving them to your Playlist gives you an easy place to return without having to make a fresh decision every time you listen.

Then add variety gradually. Your Playlist can hold familiar favorites while still making room for Core, Love, Vitality, and Abundance across the week.

Save adds the current track to your Playlist.

When a track is not right today

Do not force it. Stop, choose another soundscape, and notice what you would rather hear. There is no requirement to finish a track that does not fit the moment.

Your preferences can change from day to day. The aim is a listening relationship you can return to, not a test of endurance.

Open your Playlist
