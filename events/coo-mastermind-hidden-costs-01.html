The premise

Revenue gets applause. Margin funds the company.

In today’s business climate, the fastest path to more breathing room may not be another campaign. It may be the expense line nobody has challenged in eighteen months.

$

Processing rates creep. Insurance renews. Shipping tables age. Payroll systems multiply. Small operational compromises become permanent because everyone is busy growing.

For three days, we put the full operating stack on the table. No vague panels. No vendor parade. Just a disciplined look at where money leaves, why it leaves, and what deserves a second conversation.

The cost map

Nothing is “just the cost of doing business.”

Every recurring expense earns its place again. We examine the contracts, habits, and inherited assumptions behind the number.

01

Processing fees

Rate structure, interchange, chargebacks, gateway overlap, and the cost of convenience.

02

Insurance

Renewals, duplicate coverage, deductibles, risk classification, and broker incentives.

03

Shipping

Zone strategy, dimensional weight, surcharges, service levels, and forgotten guarantees.

04

Logistics

3PL minimums, storage, pick-and-pack logic, split shipments, and exception handling.

05

Payroll

Role design, overtime, contractor sprawl, benefits administration, and idle complexity.

06

The rest of the stack

Software, packaging, returns, inventory, facilities, and the working capital between them.
