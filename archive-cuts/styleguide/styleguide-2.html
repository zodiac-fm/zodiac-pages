Site Design System · Extracted from the Referent Board

The world stays black and white. Color arrives as an event.

Every rule on this page traces to a frame Michael approved. Nothing here is invented taste. All copy on this page is placeholder; real language comes later, through the gated copy lanes.

01 · The World

Nine laws, none negotiable

  • The ground is near-black. Photography is black and white, hard sun, real blacks and blown whites.
  • Color is an event, one per screen. It belongs to a person or a thing, never a wash.
  • Violet is the site accent. The other three colors exist and appear only when the content is theirs.
  • People in daylight are lit: bright skin, squints, crisp cast shadows. Full silhouettes are dusk grammar.
  • Waves are drawn by code, never generated. Wave E is the only wave. Water-3 is the only water.
  • No fills or panels over photography. Type sits on the stage ground, photos breathe alone.
  • Numbers are always Switzer. The serif never touches a digit.
  • The wordmark is the real asset, cream on dark, never re-typeset.
  • No charts anywhere until the site exists and the content is decided.

Sources: r19 parasol, r20 crosswalk, r21 rose-spin and four-caps-dive, wave dose E, water-embellish-3, plus the 30-frame keeper board.

03 · The Frequencies

The wave is drawn by code, never generated

This is the Signal voice: the frequency made visible. One wave per frame, whisper dose, smoke leading with a hairline filament as its spine. It reads as smoke against light, an occluding body, not a neon line. These two frames are the tapped production references.

Wave · code engine only · smoke body ~4% coverage · 40px identity clearance · one wave per frame
Water · glint rides speculars only · one wave threads the dark · two voices may share a frame when classy

Bases come from photography; the wave layer is composited in code with numeric gates (identity untouched, hue in band, clearance held). Gen-drawn waves are banned by evidence: every attempt rendered neon.

04 · Type

05 · Photography and Captions

The photo breathes, the caption whispers

The tapped referents, hard sun and dusk. Full-width for hero moments, two-up for supporting frames. Contained or full-bleed, never boxed: photos sit directly on the stage with a 4px radius, no borders, no panels, no overlays.

Caption · Switzer 400 · 14 / 1.5 desktop · 13 mobile · cream 58% · hairline rule above · max 14 words
06 · Lists

Two list forms, nothing else

Unordered: the tick

  • Each marker is a short violet line, a quiet signal.
  • Items run twelve words or fewer.
  • Three to six items per list, never more.
Marker · 16×2px Violet Glint · text Switzer body

Ordered: the counter

  1. Numbers are Switzer, letterspaced, violet soft.
  2. A hairline separates each step.
  3. Used for sequences the reader will actually follow.

07 · Copy Segment Lengths

How long anything is allowed to be

Identical limits on desktop and mobile. The design assumes these lengths; copy that exceeds them changes the design, so it goes back to the copy lane instead.

ElementLimitRule
H14 to 9 wordsOne line at desktop, never three lines anywhere.
Subhead / lede22 words maxTwo lines max. One idea, no comma chains.
Section intro45 words maxTwo to three sentences under an H2, then stop.
Body paragraph55 words maxOne to three sentences. Air between paragraphs is part of the design.
Photo caption14 words maxOne to two lines. Names what is seen, adds one fact.

10 · In-App Screens

Real screens, shown as objects of light
