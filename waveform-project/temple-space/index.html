Yes, it matches the music.This was measured from the complete supplied 30:16 master. It was not drawn by eye and was not generated from the song name. The same source file deterministically produces the same 103-bar picture.
  1. Decode the complete track to mono 8 kHz signed 16-bit PCM.
  2. Divide the complete stream into 103 equal time windows.
  3. Measure positive p99, negative-magnitude p99, and RMS independently in every window.
  4. Apply the accepted quiet/loud stretch and local contrast.
  5. Render one translucent asymmetric halo plus one opaque RMS core per bar.

Production rules

  • ViewBox 0 0 320 26; 103 bars; width 2.2; gap 0.9; radius 1.1.
  • Keep the exact shape identical everywhere. Only scale and band paint may change.
  • Do not mirror, symmetrize, smooth, randomize, redraw, or use a synthetic fallback.
  • During playback, preserve the source bars. Apply the existing white filament behind the playhead and the existing comet at x = 320 × progress.
  • Declare SVG gradients in <defs> with stable IDs. Dynamic gradient IDs can render black on iOS Safari.

No replacement visual

Do not introduce disc, circle, cymatic, album-art, or signature imagery. Temple Space's visual identity in the player and related pages is this exact waveform.
