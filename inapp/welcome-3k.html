How to Use Zodiac.fm

Zodiac is a weekly ritual. Your week starts Monday, and everything we learned in the study is based on weekly listening — not daily.

The Weekly Target
  • 63+ minutes a week — that’s 9 a day, on average
  • 15% or more in each frequency, every week
What to Play It On
  • A real speaker ($75+), headphones, AirPods, or your car
  • Skip phone & laptop speakers and cheap Bluetooth — they can’t separate out your frequencies

More time in balance means deeper, more profound experiences, faster. The target is the floor — the average member now plays over 40 minutes a day.
