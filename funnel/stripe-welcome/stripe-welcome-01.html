How to begin

Open the app, pick music that sounds good to you and press play. Let it run while you work, walk or cook. You can meditate with it, but you do not have to.

The app lets you hear each song in Core, Love, Vitality and Abundance. My Zodiac shows how much of each you've heard this week.

A few things you might be wondering

What should I play first?

Open the app, choose music you like, and let it play. There is no required first song.

Do I need headphones?

No. Headphones, earbuds, a decent Bluetooth speaker or your car stereo work. Phone and laptop speakers lose too much sound.

What should I expect?

Most people do not notice anything dramatic while the music plays. Over time, you may notice an easier decision, a boundary that holds, or knowing sooner what deserves your energy. There is no set timeline.

What is the music selection?

There are three short pieces for a few minutes of listening, plus five longer ones from nine minutes to an hour for focus, meditation or sleep. All eight are available in each of your four frequencies, prepared in 701 frequency-specific versions.

How do I cancel my trial?

Log in to your Zodiac account on the web before your seven days end. Choose Cancel Subscription and complete the final confirmation. Your music stays available through the trial, and cancelling before it ends stops the plan charge. For help, email support@zodiac.fm.

What happens after seven days?

After your seven days, the plan you chose begins and your card is charged unless you cancel first.

How much should I listen?

Aim for about nine minutes a day on average. What counts is the week, so missing a day is fine.
